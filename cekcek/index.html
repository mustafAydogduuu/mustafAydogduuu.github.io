FAQ

My street friend purchase is missing.

Open Street Friends with the paw button and tap Restore purchases at the bottom. Purchases are tied to your Apple account.

I'm not on the leaderboard.

Sign in via iOS Settings › Game Center. Your score is the money you earn this period; the board resets every 30 days.

How do I change my ad choices?

Tracking: iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. In Europe: in the game, Settings › Privacy choices.

How do I change the language?

In the game: Settings › Profile › Language.
