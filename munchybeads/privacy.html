Privacy Policy

Munchy Beads: Pixel Puzzle · Effective October 13, 2026

Munchy Beads: Pixel Puzzle ("the app") is made by Mustafa Aydoğdu ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is handled when you play.

Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email, or any account. We do not run our own servers and we do not collect personal information ourselves.

Your game progress (levels, coins, boosters, lives) and your settings (music, sound, vibration) are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob: optional rewarded ads you choose to watch, and an occasional full-screen ad between worlds. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • device identifiers, including the Apple Advertising Identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • ad interactions (for example, which ads were shown or tapped) and app usage needed for ad delivery,
  • diagnostic information such as crash and performance data.

Google's use of this information is described in Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy and in "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services": policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

  • Tracking (iOS): the app asks for permission before the Advertising Identifier can be used. You can change this any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be less personalized.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform asks for your consent for personalized ads. You can change your choice any time from the home screen: Settings > Privacy Choices.

Children

The app is a general audience game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help.

Data retention and security

Game data stays on your device until you delete the app. Advertising data is retained by Google according to its own policies.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be on this page, with the effective date at the top.
