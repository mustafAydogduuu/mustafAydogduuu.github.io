Common questions

How do I play? Tap a caterpillar at the front of a column. It crawls out, eats the beads of its own color it can reach and comes back. A hungry caterpillar waits in a bowl until its color opens up again. Clear the whole picture to win.

I ran out of lives. One life comes back every 30 minutes, up to 5.

What do coins do? Coins buy boosters (Shuffle, Rainbow Worm) and 2x speed for a level. You can also watch an optional ad to earn coins or 2x speed.

How do I change my ad privacy choices? In the EU/UK, open Settings on the home screen and tap "Privacy Choices". Tracking permission can be changed any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app? Yes. Progress is stored only on your device.
